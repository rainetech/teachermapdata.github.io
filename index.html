Getting Started

Turn your MAP data into a plan in under a minute

No sign-in, no setup. Upload your NWEA ASG export and this dashboard instantly shows who grew, who needs support, who closed gaps, and how to group your class — all in your browser. Uploading in the fall with only one test window? The dashboard detects that and switches to a start-of-year view with tiers, norm placement and growth targets instead of showing empty growth panels.

  1. 1
    Export from NWEA MAP In MAP Growth reports, open your ASG (Achievement Status & Growth) report and download it as a CSV. In the fall, a single-term export works too.
  2. 2
    Upload it here Drag the file onto the upload box, or click to browse. Your data stays on your device.
  3. 3
    Plan with confidence Explore growth, gap closure, priority & celebration lists, then build seating groups and export or print.
Where do I find my ASG CSV? & what does this tool read?
Exporting from NWEA MAP Growth
  1. Sign in to MAP Growth and open MAP Reports.
  2. Choose the Achievement Status and Growth (ASG) report for your class and terms.
  3. Use the export/download option and select CSV.
  4. Return here and upload that file — nothing is sent anywhere.
Good to know
  • Column names are matched automatically, so slightly different exports still work. Comma, semicolon and tab separated files are all read.
  • Suppressed values (***) and missing scores are handled gracefully.
  • Fall uploads with no prior window are fully supported. When the export has a single test window the dashboard hides the growth, gap-closure and quadrant panels — which would only show blanks — and analyses the window it has: percentile distribution, instructional tiers, distance from the US grade and season norm, and RIT targets for the rest of the year. It does not matter whether your export puts the fall test in the Start or the End columns.
  • Nothing is persisted. Only your theme and motion preferences are remembered; no student data is ever written to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Not ready to export? Click Load Sample Data to explore every feature first.

Growth and Achievement

Uses NWEA growth/achievement quadrants when present, with an overall view and a subject breakdown.

What the quadrants mean High-G/High-A students are growing strongly and achieving highly. Low-G/Low-A students need the most immediate instructional attention.
Why this matters This separates achievement from growth, so a lower-scoring student with strong growth is not treated the same as a lower-scoring student with low growth.
How to read movement Improved, declined, and stayed same compare StartGrowthandAchievement to EndGrowthandAchievement for the same student-subject record.
